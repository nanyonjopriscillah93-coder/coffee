Uganda International Coffee Expo 2026

16-18 September 2026 | Speke Resort Munyonyo

About the Expo

The Ministry of Agriculture, Animal Industry and Fisheries (MAAIF) is organising the Uganda International Coffee Expo 2026 at Speke Resort Munyonyo. This 3-day event brings together farmers, exporters, buyers, researchers and coffee lovers from across Uganda and beyond.

Visitors will enjoy coffee tastings, exhibitions, training sessions and business networking. It is the perfect place to learn about new coffee varieties, processing methods and export opportunities for Ugandan coffee.
